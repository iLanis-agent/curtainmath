The window

Measure the glass frame width, not the existing curtain. "Rod to floor" is from where the rod WILL hang - ideally 4-6 in above the frame, not from the frame itself. High rods make short rooms look taller.

The look

Fullness is fabric width divided by window width. A "2x" look needs 2x the fabric - one 50 in panel on a 48 in window is not a curtain, it is a flat sheet with hardware.

Your order

Panels are sold individually but hung in pairs - this app always rounds to a symmetrical pair count. Rings, clips, tiebacks and the rod itself are separate. Wash or steam before judging the length: fabric shrinks and hangs truer after the first cleaning.

What the product photo will not show you

The photo is two windows of fabric on one windowevery styled product shot uses more panels than the listing suggests - that drape is the fullness, not the fabric
"Fits up to 48 in" fits nothinga rod at its exact max span sags in the middle within a year; buy the next size up and cut the slack at the finials
Stack-back steals lightopen panels eat 15-25% of the window unless the rod extends about a foot past the frame on each side
Grommets need extra rodgrommet panels ride an inch above the rod; account for it or your kiss-length curtain floats
Blackout is a lining, not a colordark fabric is not blackout fabric - check for a foam or woven lining if sleep is the goal
Puddles collect everythinga puddled hem is a dust mop with good PR; with pets or robot vacuums, float it
